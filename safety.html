Alt accounts

Linked
  • ✓Up to 5 accounts per person, attached to one identity.

Name sniping

Blocked
  • ✓Released names enter a randomised claim window, not a race.
  • ✓Renames carry a cooldown so a name cannot be cycled for profit.
  • ✓High-value names go through review before transfer.
