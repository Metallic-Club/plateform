Account verification

Enforced
  • ✓An account that is not verified within one month of creation is removed.
  • ✓Reminders go out on day 7, 21 and 28 — deletion is never silent.
  • ✓A 7-day recovery window follows deletion, then the data is purged.
Owner's list says "a month for the timer"; the exact trigger for "verified" is still open.

Alt accounts

Linked
  • ✓Up to 5 accounts per person, all attached to one identity.
  • ✓A ban on one account bans every linked alt at the same moment.
  • ✓Shared devices and payment methods surface as link signals, never as auto-bans.
The owner's wording is "5 accounts as alts minium" — most likely a maximum; confirm before build.

Name sniping

Blocked
  • ✓Released names enter a randomised claim window instead of a race.
  • ✓Renames carry a cooldown so a name cannot be cycled for profit.
  • ✓High-value names go through review before transfer.

Moderation flow

Human in the loop
  • 1Player report, or an automated flag with its evidence attached.
  • 2Triage within 24h; low-risk cases are resolved by policy, not opinion.
  • 3Outcome is written to the account with the rule that was applied.
  • 4Appeal opens a second reviewer, never the original moderator.
